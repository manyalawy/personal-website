# Personal Portfolio Website

## What This Is

A personal portfolio website for a full stack engineer that showcases professional skills, work experience, education, and projects through playful interactive elements and smooth animations. Built with Next.js for SEO optimization, the site serves as an impressive digital presence for recruiters, potential clients, and fellow engineers.

## Core Value

Visitors should feel amazed and impressed by the craft and interactivity. Every interaction should demonstrate technical skill while remaining professional enough for recruiters, trustworthy for clients, and impressive to fellow engineers.

### Out of Scope

- Blog or writing section — keeping focus on portfolio content only
- Admin panel or CMS — content updates via code are acceptable
- Detailed project case studies — simple cards are sufficient for v1
- Real-time features or dynamic data — static/SSG approach is fine
- Video backgrounds or heavy 3D — playful doesn't mean overwhelming
- Multiple language support — English only for v1

## Context

**Audience considerations:**
- Recruiters need to quickly understand skills and experience (professional, scannable)
- Clients need to trust the quality and capability (polished, impressive)
- Fellow engineers should appreciate the technical craft (creative, well-built)

**Content status:**
- All content is prepared and ready to integrate (skills, experience, education, projects list)
- Focus can remain on building the experience rather than content creation

**Aesthetic direction:**
- Playful & interactive chosen over minimal/corporate or experimental/3D
- Hover effects, click surprises, and responsive interactions
- Smooth, polished feel that demonstrates attention to detail

## Constraints

- **Tech stack**: Next.js (required for SEO benefits), TypeScript preferred
- **Quality bar**: Ship when personally amazed, not rushing to minimum viable product
- **Aesthetic**: Playful & interactive — balance creativity with professionalism
- **Performance**: Animations must not compromise load time or interaction responsiveness
